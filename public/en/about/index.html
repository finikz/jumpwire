What is JUMPWIRE?

JUMPWIRE opened in May 2022 in a Lingnan heritage house in Fenyangli, Haizhu, Guangzhou. It is a craft beer taproom, cultural space and community hub where people come together through craft beer, films, music, conversations and events.

“Nine Dimensions” suggests a many-layered space and echoes the Chinese phrase for “long time no see”—the feeling of meeting an old friend or getting to know someone new. A jump wire connects parts of an electronic circuit. One connection can bring an unexpected sound or idea to life. Our saying is: A spark across dimensions.

This is more than a bar. Craft beer is an invitation, the old house is the setting, and the connections between people are what keep the story going.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is JUMPWIRE?

JUMPWIRE opened in May 2022 in a Lingnan heritage house in Fenyangli, Haizhu, Guangzhou. Since then, we have brought people together through beer, films, music, conversations and events. “Nine Dimensions” suggests many perspectives and echoes the Chinese phrase for “long time no see.” A jump wire connects parts of a circuit; we hope each new connection can bring an unexpected idea to life.

Where are you?

No. 5 Fenyangli, Nantian Road, Haizhu District, Guangzhou. The space is in Sishenhao Creative Park at Nos. 5 and 7 Fenyangli.

When are you open?

Sunday–Thursday, 8 pm–2 am; Friday and Saturday, 8 pm–3 am. Event and venue arrangements can change, so check with us before travelling.
