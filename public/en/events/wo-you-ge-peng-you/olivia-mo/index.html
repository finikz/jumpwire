How Do We Keep AI from Taking Our Jobs?

For episode 6 of A Friend of Mine, guest Olivia Mo joins us to talk about AI, robotics, data and changing careers—and how ordinary people can respond to a fast-moving technology wave.

September 11, 2026 · 21:00

Meet the guest

Olivia Mo has worked across robotics development and applications, big-data analytics, data mining, university teaching, research management, cloud business and state-owned enterprise management. She has also participated in the Macau Smart City think tank.

Topics for this conversation

  • Which parts of work are AI changing, beyond job titles?
  • How do robotics, data analysis and data mining show up in everyday work?
  • What skills should people build, and what human strengths should they protect?
